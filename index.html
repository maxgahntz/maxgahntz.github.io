About me

My work lives at the intersection of policy, geopolitics, and philanthropy, and a lot of it involves translating between worlds that don't naturally talk to each other: technical communities and policymakers, civil society and industry, Berlin, Brussels, and Washington, D.C.

I'm currently a Senior Advisor at the Economic Democracy Fund, a new initiative working to reclaim democratic control over vital tech infrastructures. I'm also a Senior Fellow at Mozilla, a board member of D64 — Center for Digital Progress, a Non-Resident Fellow with the Global Public Policy Institute (GPPi), and a member of the European Commission's Frontier AI Expert Forum. Previously, I spent five years building up Mozilla's global work on AI policy, shaping key parts of the EU AI Act, convening unlikely coalitions, and working across Europe, the US, and the UK. Before joining Mozilla, I worked at what is now the European Commission's AI Office as a Fellow with the Mercator Fellowship on International Affairs.

I'm based in Berlin and hold degrees in Public Policy and Administration from Columbia University, Sciences Po Paris, and the University of Konstanz.
